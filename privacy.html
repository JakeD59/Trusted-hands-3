Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. This policy explains what information Trusted Hands Appliance Repair (“we,” “us”) collects through this website and by phone or email, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you send a service request, we collect your name, phone number, the appliance type, and the description of the problem you enter. If you call or email us, we collect what you tell us. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site. This site loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when the page loads.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, schedule visits, and give quotes.
  • To perform repairs, send invoices, and handle warranty and customer service questions.
  • To keep the site secure and meet legal and tax record requirements.

Calls and texts

By sending a request, you agree that we may call, text, or email you about that request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to end texts. We do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (for example scheduling, payment, email, and website hosting), and when the law requires it.

Keeping your information

We keep your information as long as needed to serve you, honor warranties, and meet legal and tax obligations. Then we delete it or make it anonymous.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes
